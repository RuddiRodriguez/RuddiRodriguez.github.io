WEB RESEARCH / DATA QUALITY / EXCEL

Source-verified company contact enrichment

A large company workbook had missing contact details. I built a staged research process that preserved the workbook, recorded the evidence behind each update, and left uncertain fields blank.

The problem

The source contained Romanian company records spread across a master sheet and sector sheets. The task was not simply to find plausible email addresses. Each address needed a public source, the company identity had to match, and older evidence could not be presented as current.

The client stopped responding before the whole database was researched. I therefore froze the latest verified checkpoint instead of presenting partial work as a completed database.

The research method

StageWhat I checkedRule
IdentifyCompany name, phone, website, and public business referencesThe evidence had to belong to the same company.
VerifyOfficial pages first, then clearly labelled public directories or older recordsMasked, guessed, and inferred emails were rejected.
RecordEmail, source URL, evidence date, and verification noteHistorical evidence stayed labelled historical.
CheckpointTwenty rows at a time, with a separate workbook and validation reportNo batch was accepted until workbook checks passed.

The paused checkpoint

The final batch added eight publicly sourced addresses. Three came only from older public sources and were labelled historical. The final workbook passed checks for file integrity, sheet structure, styles, formulas, and consistency between the master and sector sheets.

This page describes the method and verified progress. The raw company workbook is not published because it contains collected contact data, and the project remains partial.
