WEB SCRAPING / RETAIL DATA

Walmart catalog scraper

A product page can show a price for the wrong store. I built a Python scraper that checked which store and pickup option each price belonged to before saving it.

What the scraper kept

Each product had a source URL, collection time, and separate regular and sale price fields. If pickup was unavailable, the product could stay in the sample with its price fields blank. A price from another store would have been a plausible number in the wrong row.

What the two runs showed

Each run recorded 506 distinct products across 11 categories, with no duplicate product IDs within either sample. The repeat rejected six responses: two for the wrong store and four without local pickup evidence.

The two samples shared 468 product IDs. Of those, 433 had a comparable price in both runs; all 433 matched. These were limited samples. They do not establish full catalog coverage or ongoing automated delivery.
