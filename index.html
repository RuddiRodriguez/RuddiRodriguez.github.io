FEATURED SERIES / CAMPAIGN RESPONSE MODELLING

Who is likely to respond to a campaign?

I developed the original response model in a bank. The work connected historical campaign contacts, customer and account data, a trained classifier, and batch scoring that ranked candidate records for campaign selection.

Responders were a small minority. I used lift and gain to assess how well the ranking concentrated them within different selection percentages, supporting a business decision based on contact cost and response value. Read how the ranking is evaluated. The reusable workflow documented here grew from that practical problem. The original banking setup and SQL examples use anonymous names.
